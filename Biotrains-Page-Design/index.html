our story

We are TEMPRIS, Pioneer and Leaders in Temperature Measurement system in Real time Monitoring.

Our CEO Anton Mangold founded Tempris in 2009 as he believed that the lyophilization community required innovative technology for real time monitoring of processes. Our Innovative sensor Technology was developed for in collaboration with leading pharmaceutical companies and universities in Europe and the United States.
